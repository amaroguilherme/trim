# Trim: Portfolio Optimization

Trim is a financial portfolio optimization. It solves the complex NP-Hard problem of balancing maximum asset yield against market risk and correlation, delivering mathematically precise portfolio allocations in milliseconds.

If you are building fintech apps, algorithmic trading bots, or institutional wealth management tools, Trim allows you to bypass the combinatorial explosion of traditional optimization models.

## Key Features

Risk vs. Yield Balancing: Mathematically identifies and cuts highly correlated, risky assets before they can damage a portfolio's yield.

Combinatorial Precision: Maps your assets into a QUBO (Quadratic Unconstrained Binary Optimization) matrix to find the absolute most stable subset of investments.

Active Learning Loop: The engine continuously learns from the exact mathematical outputs, making its neural network intuition faster and sharper over time.

## Use Cases
Robo-Advisors: Generate personalized asset allocations for B2C users in real-time.

Quant Trading: Rebalance massive institutional portfolios on the fly to react to sudden market correlations.

Risk Management SaaS: Provide instant "what-if" analysis for highly correlated market scenarios.

## Security & Infrastructure

Trim is hosted on a secure infrastructure. Your proprietary market data and correlation matrices are processed in-memory for the duration of the request and are never stored or used to train public models.
